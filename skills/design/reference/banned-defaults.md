# Banned AI-look defaults

Generic looks a model reaches for when nothing in the brief says otherwise. Each is banned unless DESIGN.md or the brand assets explicitly choose it and say why. Defaults drift as models and trends change: every entry carries the date it was noted, and the list is reviewed (re-dated, cut, or extended) whenever it is touched.

| Default | Noted | Why it is banned |
|---|---|---|
| Purple or indigo to blue gradients as the brand color | 2026-10-28 | The single most common model default; says "generated", not what the product is. |
| Glassmorphism cards (blur, translucent white, hairline border) | 2026-10-28 | Hurts contrast over busy backgrounds and is applied for style, not function. |
| Beige or cream everything, with a serif display face | 2026-10-28 | The current "tasteful" default; a palette that fits no brand in particular. |
| Emoji as icons or bullets | 2026-10-28 | Renders differently per platform and reads as unfinished; use Phosphor or Simple Icons. |
| Stock 3D blobs, orbs, and floating abstract shapes as hero art | 2026-10-28 | Decoration that carries no product information. |
| Gradient text on headings | 2026-10-28 | Weakens contrast and legibility; emphasis should come from weight and size. |
| Every container a rounded-2xl card with a soft drop shadow | 2026-10-28 | Flattens hierarchy: when everything is a card, nothing is grouped. |
| Centered hero, three feature cards, then a centered call to action | 2026-10-28 | The default landing layout; it ignores the product's real structure. |
| Glowing dark mode with neon accents on near-black | 2026-10-28 | Fails contrast and eye comfort at length, and signals "AI product" by reflex. |
| Decorative sparkle, wand, or star icons for AI features | 2026-10-28 | Marks a feature as AI instead of saying what it does. |
| Inter or system sans at a single weight for every role | 2026-10-28 | No typographic hierarchy; pair roles deliberately. |

A critic or author that finds one of these in a draft returns a finding (verdict: change) naming the entry, unless the choice is justified in DESIGN.md.
