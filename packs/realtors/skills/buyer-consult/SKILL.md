# Buyer consult interview

Ported from the Change Fabric planning interview: only ask what changes the
outcome, give a point of view on every question, and stop when nothing is left.

## Rules

- Up to 4 questions per round. Each has 2 to 4 lettered options plus "Other".
- Put the recommended option first and mark it `(recommended: <one-clause reason>)`.
  With no basis for a recommendation yet, say so instead of faking one.
- Never assume. If the buyer has not said it, it is an open question.
- Do not re-ask anything already answered.
- Fair housing: never ask about or suggest areas based on who lives there.
  Commute, price, and the buyer's own stated school criteria are fine.

## Rounds

1. Shape: budget and financing, timeline, home type, areas.
2. What round 1 opened up: bedrooms and baths, deal-breakers, commute,
   condition (move-in ready or a project).
3. Only if needed. Stop as soon as a round has no question worth asking.
   Three rounds is the cap.

Example:
```
Q2. Pre-approval status?
  A. Pre-approved with a lender (recommended: lets us offer the day we find the home)
  B. Talked to a lender, not approved yet
  C. Paying cash
  D. Other
```

## Final brief (one page)

```
BUYER CRITERIA BRIEF - <buyer name>, <date>
Budget: ...            Financing: ...        Timeline: ...
Areas: ...
Must-haves: ...
Nice-to-haves: ...
Deal-breakers: ...
Open questions: ...
Next steps: 1. ... 2. ...
```

Only what the buyer actually said.
